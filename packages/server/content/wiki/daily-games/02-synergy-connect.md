---
title: Synergy Connect
description: Sort sixteen cards into four groups, each built around a legendary creature.
---

Synergy Connect deals sixteen cards in a 4×4 grid. Hidden among them are four groups of four, and
your job is to find them. Each group belongs to a legendary creature: the four cards are ones that
synergize strongly with that legend. The legends themselves aren't among the sixteen. Play it at
[cubecobra.com/synergyconnect](/synergyconnect), or from **Dailies** in the **Explore** menu.

## The theme

All four legends in a puzzle share a theme, shown in a line above the board: "Today's groupings
are all legendary creatures in Azorius colors." Themes come in four kinds:

- one color, like "in mono-red", or "that are colorless";
- a two-color pair, named by its guild ("in Golgari colors");
- a three-color combination, named by its shard or wedge ("in Jund colors");
- a less common creature type ("that are Minotaurs").

The legends are picked from the most-cubed legendary creatures that fit the theme, so they're
usually recognizable.

## What synergy means here

Synergy comes from the [machine-learning model](/wiki/machine-learning) behind Cube Cobra's card
recommendations and draft bots. It isn't a measure of how often two cards appear together; that
would make staples like [[Lightning Bolt]] synergize with everything. Cards score high synergy
with each other when they belong to the same archetype, form a combo, or share something else the
model has picked up on.

Each puzzle is built by choosing four legends that fit the theme and have low synergy with each
other, so the groups stay distinct. Each group's four cards are high-synergy cards for its legend
that don't also show up as high-synergy cards for the other three.

The **How to play** link at the top of the game opens these rules in a window. It also opens on
its own the first time you play in a browser.

## Making guesses

- Click a card to select it, and click it again to deselect. Hover over a card to see it full size.
- With exactly four cards selected, press **Submit**.
- **Shuffle** rearranges the cards you haven't grouped yet, which can help you see them fresh.
- **Deselect all** clears your selection.

A correct guess moves the four cards to the top of the board, framed in the group's color, with
the legend's name across the row. The colors (yellow, green, blue, purple) only tell the groups
apart; they don't rank difficulty.

A wrong guess shakes the cards and costs a life. The dots next to the theme line show how many of
your four lives are left. The message under the board tells you how close you were: "One away…"
means three of your four cards belong to the same group, and "Not a group." means fewer did. The
game doesn't stop you from submitting a combination you've already tried, and a repeated wrong
guess costs another life.

Logged in, every guess is saved as you make it, so you can leave and come back. Anonymous, your
progress is kept in your browser.

## After the game

The game ends when you've found all four groups ("Solved with 1 mistake!") or used your fourth
life ("Out of lives."). If you run out of lives while logged in, the groups you missed are filled
in with their legends' names, so you can see the answer. Anonymous players who run out of lives
don't get the answers.

Below the board is your guess history: one row of four colored squares per guess, each square
showing which group that card actually belonged to. **Copy results** copies the date, your result,
and that grid of squares, plus a link to the game.

## Stats

Logged-in players get four stats, shown under the result on today's puzzle and at the top of the
archive:

- **Current Streak** and **Best Streak** count consecutive days you've made at least one guess on
  the day's puzzle. Finishing it isn't required. See
  [Streaks and stats](/wiki/daily-games#streaks-and-stats) for the rules all three puzzle games
  share.
- **Days Played** counts every puzzle you've made a guess on, archive puzzles included.
- **Perfect Days** counts puzzles you solved without a single mistake.

## The archive

The **Archive** link at the top of the game leads to the Synergy Connect Archive: every puzzle so
far, newest first, 30 at a time. Each row shows the date and that day's theme. If you've played it,
the row also shows your result ("Solved · 1 mistake", "Out of lives", or "2/4 in progress") and a
colored square for each group you found. Otherwise it says **Play →**.

Opening a past puzzle plays it normally, marked "archive puzzle" beside its date. It counts toward
Days Played and Perfect Days but not your streak, and its result leaves off your stats.
