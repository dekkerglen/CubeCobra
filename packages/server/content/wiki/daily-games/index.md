Cube Cobra runs four free daily games. Three are puzzles with streaks and stats; the fourth is a
daily pack-one-pick-one vote. Each has an archive of every past day. The three puzzles can be
played without an account, but logging in is what keeps your streaks and results.

## The games

- [Mana Matrix](/wiki/daily-games/mana-matrix) is a 3×3 grid: three card categories across the top,
  three down the side, and you name a card that fits both categories of each cell. Check as often
  as you like; the score is how many cells you solve and how many checks it took.
- [Synergy Connect](/wiki/daily-games/synergy-connect) deals sixteen cards that hide four groups of
  four. Each group is built around a legendary creature the cards synergize with, and four wrong
  guesses end the game.
- [Crossword](/wiki/daily-games/crossword) is a timed crossword built from Magic vocabulary: card
  names, creature types, keywords, rules text. It grows from 9×9 on Monday to 15×15 on Sunday.
- [Daily P1P1](/wiki/daily-games/daily-p1p1) shows a pack from one of the site's featured cubes and
  asks what you'd take first. You vote, then see how everyone else voted.

## Where to find them

The **Explore** menu in the top navigation has a **Dailies** section with all four games, in the
order above. On a phone, the same four links are in the navigation drawer.

The three puzzles also have short addresses, which are what their share buttons link to:
[cubecobra.com/manamatrix](/manamatrix), [cubecobra.com/synergyconnect](/synergyconnect), and
[cubecobra.com/crossword](/crossword).

### The dashboard card

When you're logged in, your dashboard shows a card for one of today's dailies that you haven't
started yet, with a preview: the empty Mana Matrix grid, the sixteen Synergy Connect cards, the
shape of the crossword grid, or the Daily P1P1 pack. Each card links to the game and to its
archive.

The order the games come up in is shuffled per user, once a day, so the card stays the same all
day until you start the game it shows; then the next one in your order takes its place. What
counts as starting: one check in Mana Matrix, one guess in Synergy Connect, a vote in the Daily
P1P1, and, for the crossword, just opening it. Once you've started all of them, the card becomes
**Today's Dailies**, with links to each game.

To keep a game off the dashboard, untick it under **Dailies on dashboard** in
[Account Settings → Display Preferences](/wiki/account-settings#display-preferences). With every
game unticked, the card disappears.

## When new puzzles appear

All four games roll over at midnight UTC. Each puzzle is dated by its UTC day, so in the Americas
the next day's puzzle arrives in the evening: at 7 p.m. US Eastern Standard Time, the puzzle
dated tomorrow goes live.

## Logged in or anonymous

You can play Mana Matrix, Synergy Connect, and the Crossword without an account. Anonymous
progress is kept in your browser for that puzzle, so a reload picks up where you left off, but
nothing is recorded on the site: no streak, no stats, no archive results, and on another device
you start fresh.

Logging in adds:

- streaks and stats for each puzzle game;
- your result for every puzzle you've played, shown in the archives;
- in Mana Matrix, your answers counted toward the answer percentages other players see;
- in Synergy Connect, the full answers when you run out of lives;
- in the Crossword, a recorded solve time and the **Reveal word** button.

The Daily P1P1 needs an account to vote at all. Logged out, you can look at the pack, but not
vote or see the results.

## Streaks and stats

Each puzzle game keeps its own streak: the number of consecutive days you've played that day's
puzzle. A day counts once you've done the following on its puzzle:

| Game | A day counts once you've... | Stats shown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mana Matrix | checked your answers once | Current Streak, Best Streak, Days Played, Perfect Days |
| Synergy Connect | submitted one guess | Current Streak, Best Streak, Days Played, Perfect Days |
| Crossword | checked, revealed a word, or filled the grid | Current Streak, Best Streak, Solved, Unaided, Best Time |

You don't need to finish to keep a streak going; one check or guess is enough. Only the current
day's puzzle counts, so you can't build or repair a streak from the archive. Miss a day and the
streak restarts at 1 the next time you play. The **Current Streak** number doesn't change until
then, so after a missed day it can still show a streak you've already lost.

Your stats appear under your result when you finish (or, in Mana Matrix, check) today's puzzle,
and at the top of each game's archive page. Each game page explains its own stats.

## Archives

Every puzzle game has an **Archive** link at the top of its page, leading to a list of every
puzzle so far, newest first, 30 at a time with **Load More** at the bottom. When you're logged in,
each row shows your result for that day, or **Play →** if you haven't played it.

Opening an old puzzle plays it exactly like the current one, with "archive puzzle" next to its
date and a **Today's Puzzle** link back. Past puzzles count toward your totals (days played,
perfect days, puzzles solved, best time) but never toward a streak.

The Daily P1P1 has its own archive of past packs, covered on
[its page](/wiki/daily-games/daily-p1p1#the-archive).
