title: Daily P1P1
description: One pack a day from a featured cube. Vote for your first pick, then see how everyone voted.
---

The Daily P1P1 (pack one, pick one) is a single pack from one of the site's featured cubes, dealt
fresh each day. You vote for the card you'd take first, then see what everyone else picked. There's
no score or streak; it's a daily look at a cube you might not have seen, and a question about what
the right pick is.

Open today's pack from **Daily P1P1** under **Dailies** in the **Explore** menu, or from the
dashboard card's **Make Your Pick** button when the Daily P1P1 is the game it's showing.

## Where the pack comes from

Each day at midnight UTC, Cube Cobra picks a random cube from the featured cubes queue, the same
queue that fills the **Featured Cubes** section of the dashboard. Patreon supporters can place a
cube in that queue (see
[Account Settings → Patreon Integration](/wiki/account-settings#patreon-integration)).

The pack is dealt using that cube's default draft format, so if the owner has made a custom format
the default, the pack follows its rules. Ten candidate packs are dealt, and the one kept is the
one whose best card, by [bot rating](/wiki/machine-learning#bot-ratings), rates lowest: the same
idea as the "balanced" option on a cube's sample packs, meant to avoid a pack with one obvious
pick.

If your cube is chosen, you get a notification saying it's featured on that day's Daily Pack 1
Pick 1.

## Voting

The pack opens on a P1P1 page within the featured cube's page, headed "Pack 1 Pick 1 from" the
cube's name and its owner, each linked.

- Logged in, click a card to vote for it. You can change your vote any time by clicking a
  different card; each account has one vote per pack.
- Logged out, you'll see "Log in to vote on your pick!" and the cards link to their card pages
  instead.

Other people's votes stay hidden until you've cast yours. After you vote, every card shows the
share of votes it got, with colors marking the top pick, your pick, and both at once when they're
the same card. Below the pack, **Voting Results** ranks every card that got a vote, with
its vote count and percentage, and marks yours.

**Share** copies the page's link, and **Get image** opens the pack as a single image. Each pack
has a comment section at the bottom.

## How it differs from a cube's P1P1 polls

Any cube can have P1P1 polls, made from its
[sample packs](/wiki/cube-page/playtest#sample-packs). The Daily P1P1 uses the same page and the
same voting, with a few differences:

- It's created by Cube Cobra, not a user. The cube is picked from the featured queue and the pack
  is dealt automatically.
- It's linked from the **Dailies** menu, the dashboard, and the Daily P1P1 archive. It also shows
  up in the cube's own list of previous P1P1s, like any other poll.
- It can't be deleted, even by the cube's owner.
- Polls that users create include Cube Cobra's own pick: the bot's choice counts as a vote in the
  results, and a **Show Bot Weights** button shows how the bot rated every card. The daily pack
  has neither; only people vote on it.

## The archive

The **Archive** link on the dashboard card leads to the Daily Pack 1 Pick 1 Archive, which lists
every past daily pack, newest first, ten at a time with **Load More** at the bottom. Each entry
shows the date, the cube and its owner, and the full pack, with each card linking to its card
page. **View Pack** opens that day's voting page. Voting never closes, so you can still vote on an
old pack and see its results.

When there's no daily pack to show, the **Daily P1P1** menu link opens the archive instead.
