The Crossword is a daily crossword puzzle made entirely of Magic vocabulary. It's timed, and your
result is how long the solve took and how much help you used. Play it at
[cubecobra.com/crossword](/crossword), or from **Dailies** in the **Explore** menu.

## The grid

Like a newspaper crossword, the puzzle gets bigger as the week goes on, and each weekday has a
theme of its own kind:

| Day | Size | Theme |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | 9×9 | a creature type |
| Tuesday | 10×10 | a single color |
| Wednesday | 11×11 | a card type |
| Thursday | 12×12 | a keyword ability |
| Friday | 13×13 | a mana value |
| Saturday | 14×14 | a two-color pair |
| Sunday | 15×15 | a set |

The theme shows up in the long entries that run edge to edge across the grid: card names picked
to share that day's trait, as many of them as the grid allows. The theme isn't announced; working
it out is part of the solve, and once you have it, it helps with the long answers. If no themed
grid can be built for a day, that puzzle has no theme. Days follow the puzzle's UTC date (see
[When new puzzles appear](/wiki/daily-games#when-new-puzzles-appear)).

The rest of the grid draws on all kinds of Magic words: card names, single words or pairs of words
from card names, the initials of a card name, legends' short names and titles, creature types,
keywords, set codes, and words from rules text. Each clue in the **Across** and **Down** lists ends
with its answer's length in parentheses.

## Moving around and typing

Click a square to put the cursor there; the word it belongs to is highlighted, and its clue appears
in the bar under the grid. Clicking a clue in either list jumps to that word.

- Type letters to fill squares. The cursor moves to the next empty square in the word, skipping
  letters you've already filled.
- **Backspace** deletes the letter under the cursor, or the one before it if the square is
  empty. **Delete** clears the square without moving.
- **Space**, or clicking the selected square again, switches between across and down.
- The **arrow keys** move along the current direction. An arrow key in the other direction turns
  the cursor first.
- **Tab** or **Enter** jumps to the next clue you haven't filled in, and **Shift+Tab** or
  **Shift+Enter** goes back. The arrows beside the clue bar do the same, which is the way back on a
  phone keyboard.

Clues you've filled in completely are grayed out in the lists.

## Check, Reveal word, and Clear

Three buttons sit under the grid:

- **Check** grades the whole grid. Wrong letters turn red (typing over one clears the mark), and a
  message counts what's left: "2 wrong letters, 9 still blank." Each press counts as one check.
- **Reveal word** fills in the highlighted word with the right answer, drawn in a different color
  from your own letters. Each press counts as one reveal. It requires being logged in.
- **Clear** empties every square in the grid. It doesn't reset the clock or your check and reveal
  counts.

Checks and reveals don't add time. They're listed next to your time in the result instead, and
using either one means the solve doesn't count as **Unaided**.

## Finishing

You don't need to submit anything. The moment every square is filled, the grid is graded
automatically, and this doesn't count as a check. If something's wrong, you get "Not quite —
something in the grid is wrong." without being told where. Fix it (or press **Check** to find out
where) and the grid is graded again once it's full.

When the grid is right, the clock stops, the grid locks, and the result appears: "Solved in 6:42
with 2 checks, 1 reveal.", or "with no help." if you used neither. **Copy results** copies the
date, that line, and a link to the game. Under it, the **Answers** list shows every entry by
number and direction (14A, 3D), and where the grid version differs from the real thing, the
readable form beside it: an entry like LIGHTNINGBOLT would show "Lightning Bolt" next to it.

## The clock

Logged in, your clock starts the first time you open a puzzle, and it measures real time from then
on, even while the page is closed. If you open the puzzle, leave, and come back the next morning,
that whole gap is in your time. Open it when you have time to finish. This applies to archive
puzzles too: opening one starts its clock.

Logged out, the timer only counts how long the page has been open, and nothing is recorded.

## Saving your progress

Your letters are saved in your browser as you type, logged in or not, so a reload picks up where
you left off. They aren't saved to your account: if you switch devices partway through, you start
from an empty grid (and, logged in, the clock keeps running). Once you've solved a puzzle while
logged in, it shows its finished grid on any device.

## Stats

Logged-in players see these stats under the result on today's puzzle and at the top of the
archive:

- **Current Streak** and **Best Streak** count consecutive days you've checked, revealed a word, or
  filled the grid on the day's puzzle. Opening it and typing a few letters isn't enough, but
  solving it isn't required. See [Streaks and stats](/wiki/daily-games#streaks-and-stats) for the
  rules all three puzzle games share.
- **Solved** counts every crossword you've solved, archive puzzles included.
- **Best Time** is your fastest solve of any puzzle, archive included.
- **Unaided** (archive page only) counts solves with no checks and no reveals.

## The archive

The **Archive** link at the top of the game leads to the Crossword Archive: every puzzle so far,
newest first, 30 at a time. Each row shows the date and the grid's size and clue count, like
"12×12 · 64 clues". If you've solved it, the row shows your time and any checks or reveals; if
you've started but not solved it, it says "In progress"; otherwise, **Play →**. A puzzle you've
only opened, without checking, revealing, or filling the grid, still reads **Play →**.

Opening a past puzzle plays it normally, marked "archive puzzle" beside its date. It counts toward
Solved, Unaided, and Best Time but not your streak, and its result leaves off your stats.
